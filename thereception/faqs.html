Fabulously Apt Questions

Click on a category to view the FAQs!

What should I wear?

This is a formal event, but above all else, we want you to be comfortable!

I have a dietary requirement, will the pizza van at the recption be able to accomodate me?

The pizza van can accomodate different dietary needs. They have gluten-free bases and vegetarian, vegan and dairy-free toppings options. However, as the pizzas are all cooked in the same oven, we cannot guarantee that there will be no contamination. If you have a sensitive allergy or intolerance, and you believe the pizza van will not be able to accomodate you, please email us.

Where is Michelham Priory?

The venue's address is Michelham Priory, Upper Dicker, Hailsham BN27 3QS, United Kingdom. The area is fairly well connected by train - there are several train stations in the area between Lewes and Eastbourne.

Where can I stay?

There are Premier Inn and Travelodge hotels in nearby Eastbourne, Lewes, and Hellingly. Michelham Priory also has some suggestions of accommodation.

How should I get to and from the wedding?

We recommend using a local taxi firm to hire transport to and from your accomodation. When booking a taxi, please keep in mind that carriages is at 11:10pm.
